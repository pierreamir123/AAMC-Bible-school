## For future agent
Interactive 60-minute team workshop page for the Spirit axis ([[Servant Program - Stage 2 Trident]]): the team co-creates the service name, mission, culture/values/beliefs, a biblical figure as symbol, and brand identity. Source: `Deliverables/Identity Workshop/workshop.html`; published as a Claude artifact (https://claude.ai/artifact/Dw7NotvXFmqgqzDiaMKnVD, private until shared; team needs Contributor access to write and vote, Editor to run the panel). v2 (2026-11-18) reworked it after the owner's feedback. Results are not collected yet; update this note after the session.

- **Flow (global steps, not per topic):** opening 3 · individual 20 · groups 12 · vote 10 · decision 12 · closing 3 = 60 min. Interpretation: the 20 minutes of individual writing cover all five topics (about 4 min each, with a pace marker); the owner may have meant 20 per topic, which would not fit in 60.
- **Five topics, each a step-by-step guide:** mission (sentence template + word chips), values (18 options grouped by the parts of the vision, pick up to 5), biblical figure (checklist + 10 inspiration examples, St. Timothy excluded), name (8-point "how to build a brand name" checklist + inspiration), brand identity (color, symbol, tone, tagline options + checklist).
- **Group step:** people pick a group number, read everyone's individual ideas, and post one proposal per topic. Practical with at least about 6 people.
- **Michael's panel:** one big "Next" button moves everyone to the next step and starts its timer; "board" mode shows timer and results full screen; "approve" under a proposal records the decision. Panel appears for viewers with Editor access.
- **Offline:** ideas queue in localStorage and sync later; paper ideas added by bulk paste; voting needs a connection (otherwise show of hands).
- Wording rule from owner: do not say "صاحب الخدمة" in team-facing text; use "مايكل" instead of "الميسّر".
- **Not verified:** tested only locally with a stubbed database, not with several real phones or visually on a phone.
